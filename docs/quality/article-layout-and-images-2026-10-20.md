# Article layout and image rules — 20 October 2026

The reported article and both healthy examples were inspected through their real public API and rendered production pages. This was read-only. The images in the reported article all decoded successfully. Its unbroken Arabic paragraph caused intrinsic grid sizing to expand the document to **2076 px on a 390 px viewport**. Images inherited the expanded width; its extra images were also stacked as separate figures. Both healthy examples stayed at 390 px because their text contained normal word boundaries.

Implemented rules:

- Article layout columns, hero, body, related content, headings and pasted text have bounded intrinsic widths. Long unbroken text wraps without truncation, rewriting, inserting characters or rejecting legitimate article content. Paragraph/newline formatting is retained.
- The cover stays separate above the article, with a bounded 16:9 frame. Only the additional images form a horizontal carousel after the article text; the administrative preview has the same separation. At most twelve distinct gallery sources are displayed, excluding a repeated cover. Articles without additional images have no empty carousel.
- Gallery images occupy one scrollable horizontal row, with two 4:3 cards on desktop and one card plus a visible portion of the next on mobile. A lone gallery image has a bounded width. Every image uses `object-fit: contain`; portrait, landscape and very wide images do not crop, stretch or determine document width.
- The carousel supports previous/next controls, RTL/LTR keyboard arrows, Home/End and native horizontal touch scrolling with scroll snapping. Navigation buttons disappear when the whole gallery already fits and disable at the scroll edges. There is no automatic motion. Vertical scrolling/pinch zoom remain available. Gallery navigation never replaces or moves the cover into the carousel.
- Missing or undecodable gallery sources are skipped, including failures that occurred before hydration attached event handlers. The remaining cards and text stay usable. If every gallery source fails, a compact explanatory placeholder replaces the gallery. A missing cover has its own fallback and does not consume a gallery image. Unsafe source schemes are excluded; controlled blob URLs are accepted only by the administrative preview.
- Existing upload controls continue rejecting corrupt bytes, unsupported formats, MIME mismatches, oversized files, excessive pixels, animated inputs and malware before storage. Real article-upload tests confirm rejected files create no photo records and preserve the editor's entered text. Scanned images retain the existing normalization/orientation/metadata stripping pipeline.
- Article saves normalize a cover repeated in the gallery, including a cover changed to an existing gallery image. Administrative and public read projections also normalize legacy duplicate references, preventing invalid stored galleries from blocking the editor; the carousel remains defensive about repeated sources. Existing production article and image records are not deleted or rewritten by this delivery.

- Focused frontend tests cover cover/gallery separation in Arabic and English, a horizontal card track, duplicate/unsafe/missing/failed sources, empty galleries and restricted blob previews, alongside public article and administrative editor regressions.
- API regressions cover cover/gallery normalization. Existing image-validation tests cover corrupt, unsupported, oversized, mismatched and infected uploads.

It uses Chromium and WebKit at 320/390/1440 px with no intercepted responses. Synthetic records/files are cleaned afterward. Rate limiting remains enabled and the script waits for its window when needed.
